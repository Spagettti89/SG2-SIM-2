MARIP 1202 twin-waterjet catamaran · 11.5 × 6.41 m · 22.5–33 t · 2 × 119 kW · Alamarin-Jet Sigma (Twin Lever / E-Helm / 3-axis Joystick) · bow thrusters 210/240 kgf
Quest controllers
Left stick ↕ = PORT combinator (fwd = ahead, back = astern/bucket)
Right stick ↕ = STBD combinator
Right stick ↔ = steering nozzles ±30°
Left stick ↔ = bow thrusters
Right grip = link both levers · Right trigger = nozzle centre
Y = menu (scenario, control mode, wind, current, spot…) · hold LEFT GRIP + left stick = walk on deck, right stick = snap turn
Left trigger cycles Joystick ⇄ Position Hold · B (keyboard) / menu = remove the temporary cabin
Joystick mode: right stick = sway/surge · left stick ↔ = twist (rotate)
A = standing spot (fwd / stern / port / stbd) · B = reset · X = next scenario · left stick click = pause · right stick click = AUTO demo
Marina scenario: schematic of Abu Dhabi Marine (Breakwater) — pontoons A–H, entrance from the north; G41 is a finger berth on the west side of pontoon G. Layout is representative, not surveyed.
Keyboard: W/S both levers · Q/A port · E/D stbd · ←/→ nozzle · Z/C bow thruster · Space all stop · V camera · R reset · N next · M joystick mode · H position hold · I/K/J/L/U/O joystick surge/sway/twist
Waterjet notes: no steering without flow — an idle jet gives no rudder effect. The reverse deflector keeps the lateral sense of the nozzle, so astern the stern walks AWAY from the nozzle side. One jet ahead + one astern with nozzles deflected = pure sway (the joystick's trick); with nozzles amidships = pure pivot. Bow thrusters lose effect above ~3 kn.
Calibrated to trials: 6.2 kn max @22.5 t · 360° in ~58 s from 6.5 kn, final dia ~30 m · crash stop ~6 s / 9 m · nozzle 4°/s (yard steering test) · engine/bucket lag 1.5 s.
